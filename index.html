Build controls

  • Drag a part from the list onto the floor or another part
  • Drop a wheel on a motor's orange face to mount it
  • Drag empty space to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom
  • R T rotate · Q E lower / raise · Del remove
  • Space switch between Build and Run
